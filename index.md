This book extends the [T1 Mapping chapter](https://qmrlab.org/mooc/t1-mapping) of the
[Quantitative MRI mOOC](https://qmrlab.org/mooc/) [@boudreau2024]. That chapter explains
what T1 is and how it is measured in the brain. This book looks at the heart, where the same
measurement becomes much harder: why that is, how the three most common cardiac methods
(MOLLI, ShMOLLI and SASHA) work, and what can go wrong with each of them. The main source is the book chapter *Fundamentals of Cardiac T1
Mapping* [@barral2018].

Each page first explains an idea and then shows it in an interactive figure. The
"Try this" prompts under each figure tell you what to change and what to look for. Most
figures are simulations; the last one shows measured data from pig hearts.
