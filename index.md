This book extends the [T1 Mapping chapter](https://qmrlab.org/mooc/t1-mapping) of the
[Quantitative MRI mOOC](https://qmrlab.org/mooc/) [@boudreau2024]. While that chapter
focuses on the brain, this one looks at the heart: why T1 is harder to measure there, how
the three most common cardiac methods (MOLLI, ShMOLLI and SASHA) work, and what can go
wrong with each of them. The main source is the book chapter *Fundamentals of Cardiac T1
Mapping* [@barral2018].

Each section first explains an idea in the text and then shows it in an interactive
figure. The exercises at the end of each section use these figures. All data in the
figures are simulated.
